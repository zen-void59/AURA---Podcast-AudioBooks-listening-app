Why AURA?

Everything You Need.
Nothing You Don't.

Built for focused listening. Handcrafted with warm aesthetics, background audio engine, and an offline-first architecture that respects your time.

🚫

Zero Ads. Ever.

No banner ads, no audio interruptions, no tracking. AURA is free and will always be free — no strings attached.

🔒

Background Playback

Full lock-screen controls, headset pause, and seamless notification media player — your audio keeps playing wherever you go.

📚

Free Classic Audiobooks

Thousands of copyright-free audiobooks via LibriVox integration — Dostoevsky, Jane Austen, Premchand and more. All free.

🎙️

Global Podcast Discovery

Browse and stream millions of podcasts. Smart search, episode history, and chapter-level progress tracking built in.

⚡

Blazing Fast Player

Powered by media_kit and just_audio — variable speed (0.5× to 3×), sleep timer, crossfade, and millisecond-precise seeking.

🌙

Warm Cream + Dark Mode

Two hand-tuned themes: Warm Linen Cream for daylight and Obsidian Midnight for your evening listening sessions.
